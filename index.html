THE PERSON BEHIND THE PURPOSE

Strong institutions
matter.

A lifelong commitment to justice, leadership and the institutions that shape our society.

Jacqueline Wanjiku Waihenya is an Advocate of the High Court of Kenya, Chartered Arbitrator, Chartered Mediator and Governance Expert.

Her journey across the legal profession, governance institutions and the dispute resolution community has been guided by a belief in building systems people can trust.

“I believe that a strong Bar and a strong Bench are mutually reinforcing pillars for the legal profession.”
