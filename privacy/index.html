Your privacy matters. This notice explains what information may be involved when you visit Jacqueline Wanjiku Waihenya’s website, read an article or watch a video.

Information on this website

This website presents information, articles and videos. It currently has no account registration, newsletter subscription or contact form, and it does not ask you to submit personal information in order to browse.

The website itself does not currently use analytics or advertising tools, or set cookies. If these features are introduced, this policy will be updated to explain their use.

Information involved in delivering the site

When you visit a website, your browser sends technical information to the server delivering it. Depending on the hosting provider’s configuration, server logs may include your IP address, the pages or files requested, the time of your visit and information about your browser or device. Such information may be used to deliver the site, maintain its security and troubleshoot technical problems.

The website’s fonts, images, documents and video are served from the site rather than through an embedded third-party player. The hosting provider’s own practices, including log retention and server location, depend on the hosting arrangement.

Social media and external links

Links to Instagram, X and LinkedIn take you to third-party services. Those services have their own privacy policies and may collect information about your activity. We encourage you to review their policies before sharing information with them.

If you choose to contact Jacqueline through a social media platform, the information you provide may be used to respond to your message, and the platform’s privacy practices will also apply. Please avoid sending sensitive or confidential information through social media.

Your rights

Under Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019, you may have rights concerning your personal data, including the rights to be informed, request access, object to processing and seek correction or deletion of personal data, subject to applicable conditions and exceptions.

For a privacy question or request concerning this website, you can contact Jacqueline through her LinkedIn profile. You may also raise a concern with Kenya’s Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the website or its services change. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.
